What happens behind the scenes

For every message you send, Alphea:
  1. detects your emotion (frustrated, anxious, satisfied, …)
  2. scores criticality: safety, fraud, privacy, medical and legal risks escalate
  3. picks out what you're talking about (battery, delivery, support, …) and how you feel about it
